Finance and compliance

Accounting
We keep your organization's books: classifying expenses, managing regular payments and preparing financial reports.
Payroll
We prepare payroll for the church's paid staff, with particular care for pastoral compensation.
Taxes
We handle the required withholdings and submit the payments the government requires.
Audits
We coordinate financial reviews and audits by independent firms, as required by government, banks and industry.
Tax exemption
As a 501(c)(3) parent organization recognized by the IRS, we can extend coverage so your church's donations are deductible. How it works.
Insurance
We help you find the right policies to protect your property and meet public liability requirements, at the lowest possible cost.

Governance and property

Bylaws
We help you write the constitution and bylaws for your church or organization.
Pastoral search
We advise your congregation in the search for a new pastor.
Background checks
We manage criminal background checks for staff and volunteers, for example in children's ministry.
Operating procedures
From security to administration, we prepare current manuals and procedures for every area of your ministry.
Real estate and financing
Legal and financial guidance for buying or selling sanctuaries and other buildings. We prepare your loan package, present it to several lenders and help with capital campaigns.
Building projects
Guidance and oversight for building or remodeling sanctuaries and schools, including construction and occupancy permits.

Where to start

Most churches begin by joining the Network, which includes 501(c)(3) coverage and credentials. Other services are added as you need them.

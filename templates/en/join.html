Membership benefits

Tax exemption
Coverage of your church's financial activity under section 501(c)(3), so donations are tax-deductible. Learn more.
Retirement plan
Your ministers can take part in the 403(b) Clergy Retirement Plan. Learn more.
Credentials
Credentials for two ordained and two commissioned or licensed ministers per congregation.
Background checks
For volunteers serving in key ministries, such as children's ministry.

Preferred rates on

  • Group health, property, accident and disability insurance
  • Retirement plans
  • Estate planning and living trust seminars
  • Institutional, real estate and estate tax guidance and services
  • Bookkeeping and audits by independent firms

The admission process

  1. Download the application

    Choose a category: regular membership for churches and ministries, group membership for affiliated churches, or church in formation.

  2. Send it complete

    Email it to {{ data.ORG.apply_email }}. Incomplete applications cannot be processed.

  3. Review our documents

    We will send you the NCCMN constitution and bylaws before a phone interview. Every application is considered carefully and prayerfully.

  4. Welcome to the Network

    Once accepted, your church receives coverage, credentials and the Network directory.

We serve on two fronts

Your church

We take care of the administration that government, banks and insurers require.

  • Accounting, payroll and taxes
  • Financial reviews and independent audits
  • Property and liability insurance
  • Bylaws, real estate and building projects
Services for churches

Those who minister

We stand with pastors and leaders in what their calling requires under the law.

  • Recognized ministerial credentials
  • A 403(b) retirement plan for clergy
  • Health plans and professional liability insurance
  • Guidance on negotiating pastoral compensation
Services for ministers

What your church receives as a member

501(c)(3) tax exemption
NCCMN is a parent organization recognized by the IRS. Your church is covered, and your donors can deduct their tithes and offerings.
How the coverage works
Credentials for four ministers
Two ordained and two commissioned or licensed ministers per congregation, plus background checks for key volunteers.
A clergy retirement plan
Access to the Network's 403(b) Retirement Plan, and preferred rates on insurance, estate planning and bookkeeping.

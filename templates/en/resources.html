Film Forum (Cine Foro)

A film forum is a group that discusses what movies show us, in the light of Scripture. The group watches a film on a Friday or Saturday night while a leader pauses at key scenes, takes home readings for the week, then meets again to talk through an interactive presentation. Sessions are in Spanish.

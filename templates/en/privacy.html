What we collect

This site only receives what you type into our contact and prayer request forms: your name, email, phone, church and message. We do not ask for or store payment details; online gifts are processed off this site by the payment service you choose.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses no cookies, has no user accounts and runs no tracking or advertising scripts. Thumbnails in the training library load from YouTube's image servers; when you open a video you leave this site and YouTube's or Vimeo's policies apply.

How we use it

We use what you send only to answer you and, for prayer requests, to pray for you. We share a request with the prayer team only if you allow it on the form. We never sell or rent your information.

Where it is kept

Messages are stored by this site's hosting service and emailed to NCCMN staff. We delete messages we no longer need.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete what you sent by writing to {{ data.ORG.email }} or calling {{ data.ORG.phone }}.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

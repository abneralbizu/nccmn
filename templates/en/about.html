Why we exist

Many independent churches, especially Hispanic congregations, carry accounting, taxes, insurance and legal requirements on their own. That weight takes time from the pastor and energy from the mission.

NCCMN exists to carry that load alongside them. That is why we say that in the Network administration serves the mission: sound financial and legal practices strengthen ministry and protect the congregation.

Administra mi Iglesia

Administra mi Iglesia (“Manage my Church”) is the NCCMN division that provides administrative services to local churches, denominations and religious organizations: accounting, payroll, taxes, audits, insurance and more. See all services.

A network of churches

Our staff serve from several parts of the United States, and our main office is in Winter Springs, Florida. Affiliated churches and ministries are located in Connecticut, New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Florida and Texas. See the affiliated organizations.

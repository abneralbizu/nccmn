Why it matters

Only churches and religious institutions covered under section 501(c)(3) can assure their donors that gifts will be deductible on their U.S. tax returns.

If your church is not covered, your donors may not qualify for the deduction. Claiming an improper deduction can also trigger an IRS audit of the church or ministry.

Churches and ministries that are not properly recognized as exempt can face administrative and even criminal charges, with fines that can be significant for each improper claim.

How NCCMN helps

The National Christian Churches & Ministries Network is a parent (master 501(c)(3)) organization recognized by the Internal Revenue Service. When you join the Network, your church or ministry comes under that coverage, so your church's financial activity and the gifts it receives are covered by the exemption.

As part of membership we also help you keep the internal controls and records the exemption requires.

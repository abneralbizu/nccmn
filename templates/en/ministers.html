Credentials
Through NCCMN you can obtain ministerial credentials that recognize you as a religious leader before government and industry. Credentials are also required for certain tax benefits available to ministers.
Pastoral compensation
We advise you as you negotiate your compensation, so you receive a fair income that fits your ministerial role.
Health plans
We help you arrange health coverage for yourself, your family or a group from your local church, plus quotes for life, disability and long-term care insurance.
Retirement
You can take part in the Network's 403(b) Clergy Retirement Plan. Plan details.
Liability insurance
We help you obtain policies that protect you from claims of professional negligence (malpractice) and for your service on boards of directors.

Types of credentials

Each member congregation receives credentials for two ordained ministers and two commissioned or licensed ministers. Additional credentials are available at a separate cost.

The membership application covers credentials such as independent minister, evangelist, missionary, worship leader, minister of music and Christian worker. Personnel evaluations, background checks and psychological profiles are kept confidential and shared only with authorized board representatives.
